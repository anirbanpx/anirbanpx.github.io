01 About

The story so
far.

Spent 8 years as a full-stack software engineer — shipping Walgreens' merchandise planning system at 9,000-store scale, saving CVS $46M in gross margin with a pharmacy inventory product, and building enterprise IAM at CA Technologies for NASA and US Federal clients. Engineering taught me that the hardest product problems are always platform problems.

IIM Calcutta gave me the language to talk about those problems at a business level. I left with an MBA and joined UnitedHealth Group, where I built content.werally.com — Rally Health's wellness content product — from 0→1 to 100K+ monthly active members in year one.

Then Pega offered me the founding PM seat on Launchpad — a brand-new AI-powered, low-code B2B SaaS platform. Four years later: 20+ ISVs in production, Pega's AI Agent low-code builder shipped, and 90% of new expressions inside Launchpad apps are now AI-generated. Currently building TripSathi on the side — a multi-agent AI travel planner — to keep my hands in the technical stack.
